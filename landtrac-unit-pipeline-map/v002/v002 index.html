Landtrac Unit Pipeline — V002: DIV1 Loop Closed (Future State)

Goal: remove DIV1 entirely. landtrac-unit-upload writes polygons to Hendrix (not the Div1 loader); daapUnitID is minted by a new Hendrix sequence/table; producer well-enrichment moves to Databricks Unity Catalog (⚠ proposed, not yet confirmed). Green = new or changed vs. today.

What changes vs. today

DIV1 removed — loop closed. No repo reads or writes DIV1. The Div1 box, the hotfolder loader (\\prod-loader05), and the tblWell/tblDaapUnit round-trip are all gone.
landtrac-unit-upload → Hendrix. QC'd new/corrected polygons write straight to the Hendrix UNIT_POLYGON SDE instead of the Div1 hotfolder. Removes the ArcGIS shapefile export + copy step.
daapUnitID minted in Hendrix. A new Hendrix sequence/table assigns daapUnitID when a polygon lands — replacing Div1's on-import minting. daapID-update's cross-DB copy becomes an internal Hendrix operation (or is retired).
Producer enrichment → Databricks Unity Catalog. The Div1 database class and all sql/div1/* queries (tblWell + 6 tables) are replaced by reads from Unity Catalog. WellID stamping in direct-access-unit-point-feed / wellid-updates re-sources from the same.
⚠ Open decisions. (1) Unity Catalog as the well-data source is proposed, not confirmed. (2) Whether wellid-updates / daapID-update are re-sourced or retired outright once daapUnitID is native to Hendrix and WellID comes from Unity Catalog.
Unchanged. Everything from the Airflow producer rightward — Kafka dp.pres.landtracunit.v3, DSM, the Prefect flow, and all 6 publish targets — is untouched.

Column mapping: div1.dbo.tblWell → Databricks Unity Catalog

Source table: enverus.direct_access_v3.foundations_wellheader (6,184,921 rows, prod-published Direct Access V3). Delta-sharing mirror for consumers outside the enverus catalog: cts_deltasharing_da_prod.direct_access_v3.foundations_wellheader.

Match key: the pipeline joins on API10, not on WellID. UNIT_POINT_PROD.API = LEFT(ApiNo,10); DA V3 equivalent is LEFT(API_UWI_Unformatted,10). DIV1 WellID was only ever an internal backfill value and is a different id space from DA V3 WellID — do not swap it column-for-column.
